FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Are you really open 24 Hours a day?
Yes! Gaming Vault is open 24/7, 7 days a week including weekends and holidays. Whether you want an all-nighter gaming session or a late night snooker match, our doors are open.
How many controllers are included with PS5 rooms?
Every PS5 private room booking includes 2 original DualSense wireless controllers. If you have a group of 3 or 4 players, you can add extra controllers for just +150 PKR/hour.
Do I need to reserve in advance or can I walk in?
Walk-ins are always welcome! However, for VIP suites like The Vault or peak evening hours, we strongly recommend reserving a slot in advance via our dedicated booking engine (`book.html`).
What games are installed on the PS5 consoles?
We have all major AAA multiplayer and sports titles pre-installed, including EA Sports FC / FIFA, Tekken 8, Call of Duty, Mortal Kombat, GTA V, Rocket League, and It Takes Two.
